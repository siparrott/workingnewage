# Blog Writing Guidelines — local-authority + conversion

Distilled from an expert review of the family-studio post (scored 8.5 → 9.5/10).
These are baked into the idea-mode writer (`server/services/blogIdeaWriter.ts`)
and should be checked on every post — AI-drafted or hand-written.

The goal: posts that read like **13 years of real studio experience wrote them**,
not a generic AI SEO article. That voice is the one thing competitors can't copy.

## The checklist

1. **Lead with trust + observed experience.** Open with a real pattern, not a claim:
   *"Die meisten Familien sagen uns…"*, *"Nach über 13 Jahren wissen wir…"*. Weave
   in 13+ Jahre / 300+ Shootings / 4,8 ★ as lived experience, not advertising.

2. **Take a position (have an opinion).** A brand with a viewpoint is stronger than
   "we offer studio *and* outdoor". Defend the studio choice for families —
   *"kontrollierte Bedingungen statt Zufall durch Licht und Umgebung."* Include at
   least one "only New Age" element (daylight studio, deliberate studio choice).

3. **One personal/founder paragraph.** The single biggest E-E-A-T lift. A short
   first-person note from the photographer's chair showing real experience (no
   invented name — use "ich/wir" or a name supplied in the brief).

4. **Search-focused H2s.** Phrase headings as the questions people actually Google,
   with keyword + city:
   - ❌ "Vorbereitung & Outfits" → ✅ "Was anziehen für Familienfotos im Studio Wien?"
   - ❌ "Häufige Fragen" → ✅ "Häufige Fragen zum Familienshooting in Wien"

5. **Add a comparison table when there's a decision** (Studio vs Outdoor, package A
   vs B, …). Two columns, short phrases. Tables get lifted into Google snippets and
   AI answers (ChatGPT/Gemini/AI Overviews).

6. **Capture adjacent keywords, then redirect.** Name the things people search —
   real Vienna places (Schönbrunn, Stadtpark, Prater, Donauinsel, Naschmarkt) and
   "Outdoor" variants — then steer the logic back to the studio strength. Don't
   accidentally sell the reader on going elsewhere.

7. **Strong local entities.** Reference recognisable landmarks/areas (Naschmarkt,
   Margareten, 1050, U4 Kettenbrückengasse), not just the street name.

8. **Emotional close before the CTA.** Sell the emotion, not the appointment:
   *"Kinder werden größer, Familien verändern sich – aber ein gutes Portrait
   bleibt."* Then the action links.

9. **SEO title that catches multiple variants.** e.g. *"Die schönsten Locations für
   Familienfotos in Wien – Studio oder Outdoor?"* wins Location + Studio + Outdoor
   searches at once.

10. **Keep the wins we already have.** Tight internal linking (pillar + 1–2
    siblings), a real FAQ block (AEO/JSON-LD), direct answers, ~700–900 words, clean
    semantic HTML, real photos with descriptive alt.

## Why it works (GEO / AI search)
AI answer engines favour pages with location + years of experience + reviews +
direct answers + FAQs + comparison tables. We have all of those — leaning harder
into lived-experience voice and comparison tables is what moves a post from "good
SEO blog" to "the page the AI cites."
